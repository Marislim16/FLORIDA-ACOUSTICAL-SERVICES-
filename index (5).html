Metal Framing

Steel-stud walls, soffits, chases, and ceiling backing built plumb, square, and true to the wall-type schedule — load-bearing and non-load-bearing.

  • 20–25 ga. studs
  • 16" & 24" O.C. layouts
  • Deflection & slip-track details

Drywall Hanging

Board hung tight to pattern with fire and sound-rated assemblies where the wall type calls for it — no gaps at penetrations, no proud screws.

  • 5/8" Type X & standard
  • Single & double layer
  • UL-listed fire assemblies

Finishing

Tape, mud, and sand to the level your spec calls for — from a level 4 knockdown texture to a level 5 skim ready for high-sheen paint.

  • Levels 1–5 finish
  • Knockdown, orange peel, smooth
  • Light patch & repair work

Acoustical Ceilings

Suspended grid laid out from the reflected ceiling plan, leveled to a laser, and tiled clean — no visible sag, no mismatched cuts at the walls.

  • 15/16" & 9/16" grid
  • Standard & specialty tile
  • Seismic & wind-load bracing

How a job runs, start to closeout

The same five stages whether it's a single-suite buildout or a multi-floor shell.

  1. 01

    Walkthrough & takeoff

    We measure the space against your drawings and quantify framing, board, and ceiling grid by wall type.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    A bid broken out by trade — framing, drywall, finish, and ceiling — with lead times and a start date you can put on the schedule.

  3. 03

    Layout

    Wall types and grid are chalked and lasered before delivery, checked against the RCP so the GC can walk it first.

  4. 04

    Install

    Framing, board, finish, and grid go up in sequence, with each stage inspected before the next one starts.

  5. 05

    Closeout

    Punch list walked and cleared, space broom-clean and ready for flooring, paint, or the next trade in line.

Questions we get before a bid

If your question isn't here, it's faster to ask than to guess — see contact below.

Do you work with general contractors, or direct with property owners?
Both. Most of our volume runs through commercial GCs as a specialty subcontractor, but we also bid directly with property managers and tenants on standalone buildouts.
What size jobs do you take?
Anywhere from a single-suite tenant improvement to a multi-floor commercial shell. If it needs metal studs, board, finish, or a ceiling grid, we'll bid it.
Can you match an existing ceiling grid, texture, or tile pattern?
Usually, yes. Bring a sample, spec sheet, or photo of the existing condition and we'll confirm the grid profile and tile before bidding.
Do you carry your own insurance and bonding?
Yes. Certificates of insurance and bonding capacity are available on request — most GCs ask for this before adding us to a bid list, so we keep it current.
What's a typical lead time after a signed proposal?
It depends on scope and current material lead times on steel and grid, but we'll give you a firm start date in the proposal itself, not a range.
Do you take on residential work?
Our crews and scheduling are built around commercial work. Occasionally we'll take a larger residential drywall or ceiling scope — ask and we'll tell you honestly if it's a fit.
